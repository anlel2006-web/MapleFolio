Which account for what?

TFSA: growth and withdrawals are tax-free, so it suits your highest-growth holdings; US dividends still lose about 15% to withholding. RRSP: contributions reduce your taxable income, and US-listed ETFs avoid the 15% US dividend withholding there. Taxable: Canadian dividend stocks get a tax credit, so they fit here. US accounts (401(k), IRA, Roth IRA) are for US taxpayers; Roth withdrawals in retirement are tax-free. FHSA: for a first home; contributions are deductible and growth and qualifying withdrawals are tax-free. RESP: for a child’s education; the government adds a grant on contributions.
